When grid power fails, your emergency backup generator must roar to life instantly. That critical first rotation relies entirely on your heavy-duty electric or pneumatic **starter motor**. 

A sluggish crank or a failure to engage the flywheel ring gear during an emergency blackout can cost industrial facilities thousands of dollars in downtime losses within minutes.

### Preventing Starting Failures and Pinion Wear

Starter motor failures are frequently caused by repeated high-amperage heat soak, solenoid contact welding, or mechanical misalignment between the starter pinion and the flywheel ring gear. 

* **Inspect Ring Gear Teeth:** Always inspect the flywheel ring gear whenever you service your starter motor. Chipped or worn ring gear teeth will quickly chew up the drive pinion of a brand-new starter.
* **Check Battery Banks & Cables:** Voltage drop across corroded battery terminals or undersized cables reduces cranking RPM, preventing the engine from building sufficient compression velocity to fire.
