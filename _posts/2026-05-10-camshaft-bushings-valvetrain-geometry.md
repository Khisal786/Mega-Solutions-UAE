While the crankshaft grabs most of the glory, the **camshaft** is the silent conductor orchestrating every breathing event inside your industrial gas engine. Riding on a series of precision internal bushings, the camshaft converts rotational motion into precise opening and closing sequences for your intake and exhaust valves.

When camshaft bushings wear out, radial play increases, altering valve timing events and throwing your engine's combustion synchronization completely off beat.

### Recognizing Camshaft and Bushing Wear

* **Valvetrain Noise:** Excessive clearance creates a distinct tapping or clattering sound originating from the upper block or valley area.
* **Loss of Compression:** If bushing wear allows the camshaft to drop vertically, valve lift is reduced, choking cylinder airflow and dropping power output.

### Overhaul Best Practices

During a major engine teardown, always measure camshaft journal diameters and bushing oil clearances using precision micrometer and bore gauge sets. Replacing worn bushings restores proper oil pressure to the camshaft gallery and ensures your valve timing remains razor-sharp.
