The exhaust manifold of a gas-fueled power generator operates in a punishing furnace where temperatures routinely exceed 600 degrees Celsius. Securing a gas-tight seal between the heavy cast-iron or steel runner sections and the cylinder head requires specialized high-temperature **exhaust manifold gaskets**.

When these gaskets fail, the consequences go far beyond an annoying ticking sound—they degrade your entire plant's efficiency.

### The Impact of Exhaust Leaks

* **Lost Turbocharger Energy:** Escaping exhaust gases bypass the turbine wheel, dropping boost pressure and forcing your engine to burn more fuel to carry the same electrical load.
* **Thermal Distortion:** Hot exhaust jets blowing directly against surrounding wiring harnesses, insulation blankets, or painted block surfaces can trigger fire hazards or sensor failures.

### Ensuring a Leak-Free Seal

* **Use Multi-Layer Steel (MLS):** Avoid cheap composite gaskets that char and crumble under continuous high heat. Always opt for multi-layer stainless steel gaskets designed to expand and contract harmoniously with thermal shifts.
* **Retorque After Heat Cycles:** Following a top-end overhaul, perform a secondary torque check on manifold fasteners after the engine has gone through a few full thermal cycles.
