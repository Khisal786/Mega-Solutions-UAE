In power generation, fuel delivery must be governed with microscopic precision. Gas control valves regulate the exact volume of gaseous fuel entering the intake manifold or pre-combustion chambers relative to engine load and ambient conditions. 

When fuel dynamics become erratic, engines suffer from fuel hunting, unstable idle, and poor emission compliance.

### Eliminating Fuel Instability

Modern gas engines demand split-second actuator response times. If mechanical stiction or internal wear slows down your gas control valve, the engine controller cannot react fast enough to grid frequency shifts or sudden block-load applications. 

Using high-accuracy gas control components with rapid response electronics ensures your combustion process remains steady, efficient, and fully protected against knock conditions.
