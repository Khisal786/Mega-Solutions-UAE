## Quick answer

Independent sourcing can be a useful option when a power-plant team needs an alternative route for a correctly identified spare part. The safe way to buy is not to start with a brand name or a photograph: start with the engine model, serial number, part number, revision, quantity, required date, and the documents needed for acceptance.

## What an independent stockist can help with

An independent stockist may help a maintenance or procurement team compare available market options, consolidate a parts list, and arrange export documentation. That does **not** remove the buyer’s responsibility to confirm the exact application and specification.

For Type 3 and Type 6 work, a useful enquiry contains more than “I need a cylinder-head part.” Include the engine designation, serial number, the manufacturer part number where available, the supersession or revision, photographs of the identification plate, and whether the item is required for a planned outage or an unplanned repair.

## The five checks to make before placing an order

### 1. Identify the part before comparing prices

Ask the maintenance team to confirm the required item against the applicable engine documentation. Similar-looking items can differ by engine variant, revision, connector, material, or calibration. A quotation should make the offered part number and condition clear.

### 2. Ask what “available” means

Availability can mean physically held stock, incoming stock, or stock held by another party. Ask where the item is located, when it can be released, and whether the quoted quantity is reserved. This gives the outage planner a realistic basis for a decision.

### 3. Request the evidence needed for acceptance

The right evidence depends on the part and your quality process. It can include item photographs, labels, packing information, supplier traceability available for the item, and a clear condition statement. Agree this before payment and shipment.

### 4. Separate the part decision from the logistics decision

Confirm packaging, export paperwork, destination requirements, Incoterms, consignee details, and insurance separately. A correctly sourced part still needs a workable route to the site.

### 5. Record the transaction for the next outage

Keep the accepted part number, vendor documents, received condition, and installation outcome in the plant’s spares history. Over time, this turns an urgent purchase into a repeatable sourcing process.

## When independent sourcing is most useful

It is most useful when a team has a precise requirement, a defined acceptance process, and a need to compare supply routes. It is less suitable when the part cannot be identified, when engineering approval is missing, or when the buyer needs an OEM-only warranty or service decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an independent supplier confirm part compatibility?

An independent supplier can help compare the information supplied, but final compatibility should be confirmed by the operator using the applicable engine documentation and engineering process.

### What should I send for a Jenbacher spare-parts quotation?

Send the engine model and serial number, item/part number, revision where known, quantity, photos of labels, destination, required date, and any documentation requirements.

### Is an independent trader the same as an authorized distributor?

No. An independent trader operates separately from the manufacturer’s authorized distribution network. The distinction should be clear in procurement records and customer communications.
