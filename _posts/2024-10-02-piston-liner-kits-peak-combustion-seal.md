Inside the block of a high-output industrial gas engine, the piston and cylinder liner form the ultimate sealing interface. Together, they must contain thousands of pounds of explosive combustion pressure while moving at high reciprocating speeds with microscopic oil film tolerances. 

Achieving a peak combustion seal is essential; any compromise here leads directly to oil blow-by, crankcase pressurization, and devastating liner scuffing.

### Combating Scuffing and Blow-By

Liner scuffing occurs when the protective hydrodynamic oil film breaks down, allowing raw metal-to-metal contact between the piston skirt/rings and the liner wall. This is frequently triggered by:
* Operating with degraded or contaminated engine oil.
* Sub-optimal jacket water temperatures leading to thermal distortion.
* Fuel combustion anomalies causing localized hot spots.

To maximize the service life of your power assemblies, always install matched piston and liner kits engineered with precise metallurgical tolerances and optimized plateau-honed surface finishes that retain vital lubrication oil.
